🕐 Prayer Times

Manage prayer times manually or use automatic calculation.

⚙️ Prayer Mode

Paste the whole year's Athan times, one line per day, in this order:
Date, Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha
ACJU PDFs paste as they are (1-Jan 5:02 AM 6:25 AM 12:16 PM 3:36 PM 4:28 PM 6:06 PM 7:20 PM — with both Asr columns; pick Shafi'i or Hanafi below). Commas, tabs or spaces all work, so you can paste straight from Excel / a PDF table. Dates: 31/01/2027, 2027-01-31, 31 Jan or just 31/01 (day/month — repeats every year). Times: 04:46 or 4:46 AM; if there is no AM/PM, Dhuhr 1–9 and Asr, Maghrib, Isha are read as afternoon/evening (3:15 → 15:15). Today's Iqamah = that day's Athan + the minutes set for each prayer below.

🕌 Prayer Schedule

Edit individual prayer names, Athan times and Iqamah times. Set the minutes after Athan for each prayer and the Iqamah time follows the Athan automatically (in auto mode too); or type an Iqamah time and the minutes are worked out for you.

⭐ Special Screens

Full-screen messages for special occasions (Eid, Taraweeh, Laylatul Qadr, a lecture, a notice …). Each one shows on the chosen date(s) between the from / until times, when no Athan or Iqamah screen is up.

Changes save as you type. To preview on the display, open index.html?special (shows the first enabled one right away).
Leave the dates empty to show it every day in that time window; set only “from date” for a single day.
